What Sets RAM Collision Repair Apart

Ram truck frame repair

Heavy-duty work and towing put stress on a truck’s frame. We measure your Ram against factory specifications and restore frame alignment so it tracks straight and tows safely.

Aluminum hood and tailgate

The Ram 1500 uses aluminum in parts like the hood and tailgate. Our I-CAR Gold Class aluminum-trained technicians repair mixed-material trucks in a dedicated aluminum area.

Aluminum repair >

Ram driver-assist calibration

Most newer Ram models rely on cameras and radar for driver-assist features like automatic emergency braking and lane keeping. Ram trucks use front radar and a windshield camera for forward collision warning and adaptive cruise control. After bumper, windshield or front-end repairs we check and recalibrate these sensors so your safety systems work the way Ram designed them to.
