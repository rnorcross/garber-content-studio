Garber's Heart for Service

Giving back has always been at the heart of our business.

Giving back to the communities that support us has been part of the Garber mission since day one, beginning with Guy Garber founding the Saginaw Society for Crippled Children and organizing the Saginaw division of the United Way. Three generations on, we’re still following his example by giving back to local organizations and putting in thousands of volunteer hours each year.

Our Impact

What a family business owes the town that built it.

Garber Automotive Group gives back more than $1.5 million to local community organizations every year. We have led campaigns for the YMCA, United Way, and the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society, while Garber team members provide thousands of volunteer hours each year. If it matters to the community, it matters to us.

Our Partners

A group that acts like a hometown dealer.

Eight national nonprofits have the support of every Garber dealership. We sponsor events like Light the Night fundraiser walks, Habitat for Humanity renovations, and American Red Cross blood drives, among many more. And our team members dedicate thousands of hours of volunteer time participating in them.

On top of that, each individual dealership backs local causes its own team and customers are passionate about. If you know of a local nonprofit organization in need of sponsorship, we would love to learn more about it.
