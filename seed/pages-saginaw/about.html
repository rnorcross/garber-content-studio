About Our Dealership

Where two Saginaw legacies meet.

On October 2, 2017, Martin Chevrolet became Garber Chevrolet Saginaw, the 17th dealership in the Garber family. It brought together two third-generation Saginaw family businesses: Bill Martin’s, built on a dealership his grandfather Lowell Martin started, and Richard J. Garber Jr.’s, founded in 1907.

Garber kept what made Martin Chevrolet worth coming back to. Many of the same faces still work the sales floor and the service drive at 8800 Gratiot Road, and some of our customers have been buying here since it was Martin’s. What Garber added is a hometown company headquartered right here in Saginaw, with more accountability, more resources and the same promise it has kept since 1907.

For you, that means the full Chevrolet lineup, from Silverado and Tahoe to Equinox, Traverse and Trax, plus certified and pre-owned vehicles, financing for every credit situation, Certified Service, and our own body shop on site. Can’t find the exact Chevrolet you want on our lot? We pull from more than 25 Garber dealerships. These are just some of the reasons you’ll do better at Garber!

Our History

A century of automotive pioneering.

In 1907, most of Michigan still thought the horse and buggy were here to stay. Guy S. Garber, a 23-year-old farm-implement salesman, had a different vision – one where the automobile was the future of transportation. His passion caught the eye of Billy Durant, the founder of General Motors, who put him on the road to selling Buicks and opening dealerships across Michigan.

Guy Garber opened a Buick dealership in Saginaw in 1910, bought out the factory’s retail interest in 1912, and by 1913, he held the wholesale territory from Saginaw County all the way north to the Straits of Mackinac. His partnership with GM lasted nearly 60 years, and when he died in 1965, Garber Buick was the last Buick distributor in America.

Where the name first went over a door: R. J. Garber Farm Implements, 414 N. Washington Avenue, Lansing.

Guy Garber at work in the Saginaw office.

1907

Guy S. Garber goes to work for Buick

At William C. Durant's urging, the 23-year-old salesman becomes a Buick representative and opens a dealership in Battle Creek.

1912

Garber Buick is born

Buick leaves the retail business; Guy buys its Saginaw Valley interest. A year later, he is sole owner and one of the Buick Thirteen distributors.

1965

Guy Garber Passes Away at 81

After 58 years working in the auto industry, Guy Garber passed away at 81. Richard Garber Sr., his son, takes over as president and owner of Garber Buick.

1980

Richard J. Garber Jr.

Richard J. Garber Jr., Guy Garber's grandson, becomes president in the company's 73rd year, making him the youngest Buick dealer in the nation.

1986

One store becomes a group

Garber Automotive Group is born. Garber Nissan in Saginaw, Michigan and Garber Ford in Green Cove Springs, Florida are the first acquisitions.

New state · Florida
1996

RightWay Auto Sales and Gateway Financial Solutions

Garber Automotive Group establishes RightWay Auto Sales, a chain of used-car dealerships, and Gateway Financial Solutions, a subprime lending agency.

1997

Glendale Heights, Illinois

Garber adds Sunrise Chevrolet in Glendale Heights, Illinois. It is the group’s third state and first venture into the Chicago Metro area.

New state · Illinois
2002

The Saginaw Spirit come to the Great Lakes Bay Region

Garber brings an OHL franchise to Saginaw, becoming the Saginaw Spirit and playing at the Dow Event Center. They eventually become the 2024 Memorial Cup champions.

2011

Rochester, New York

Acura, Honda, Porsche and Audi franchise dealerships based in Rochester, New York join Garber Automotive Group, expanding the group into four states.

New state · New York
2017

Martin Chevrolet becomes Garber Chevrolet Saginaw

Garber welcomes Martin Chevrolet on Gratiot Road as its 17th dealership, joining two third-generation Saginaw family businesses. The same year, Richard J. Garber Jr. receives the Governor’s Lifetime Humanitarian Award.

2018–19

Hilton Head, South Carolina and Highland, Indiana

Two new dealerships are brought under the Garber umbrella, adding two states in two years – a Buick GMC Cadillac store in Bluffton, SC, and a Chevrolet store in Highland, IN.

Community

Giving back has always been at the heart of our business.

Giving back to the communities that support us has been part of the Garber mission since day one. Guy Garber founded the Saginaw Society for Crippled Children, helped organize the Community Chest, which later became the United Way, built the Garber Tennis Courts, and served on the Saginaw County School Board. Three generations on, Richard J. Garber Jr. has led campaigns for the YMCA, the CAN Council, and the Temple Theater, and Garber team members put in thousands of volunteer hours each year.

At Garber Chevrolet Saginaw, showing up for the community starts in our own hometown: sponsoring Saginaw-area schools, teams and organizations, supporting the events our team is passionate about, and giving to the causes our customers bring to us. If it matters to Saginaw, it matters here.
