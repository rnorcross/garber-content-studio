Good to Know

Questions people ask before they book.

What does mobile service cost?

The mobile diagnosis is free. Maintenance and repairs are priced the same as they would be at the Automall — you pay for the work and parts, not the trip. Service coupons and manufacturer offers apply unless an offer specifically excludes mobile service.

Where do you go?

Green Cove Springs, Clay County and the greater Jacksonville area. [Confirm radius — e.g. within 30 miles of the Automall.] Distance limits may apply; if you're on the edge, ask and we'll let you know.

Which vehicles qualify?

Most makes and models — Ford, Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram, and most others on the road. A few vehicles and jobs need a lift or specialty equipment; we'll tell you up front if yours is one of them.

How long does a visit take?

It depends on the job. An oil change and tire rotation is usually under an hour; brakes or a battery take longer. Your advisor will give you a time estimate when the appointment is confirmed.

Who's working on my vehicle?

The same factory-trained, certified technicians who work in our service bays on U.S. 17 — with a fully stocked service van instead of a lift.

What if it needs more than you can do on site?

You'll get a straight answer and a quote before anything happens. If the repair needs the shop, we'll help you get the vehicle to the Automall, and Garber Automall Collision Center handles body work.
