About Our Reviews

What all Garber Automall reviews have in common.

Read through Garber Automall’s reviews and you’ll find the same things coming up over and over again. Drivers from all across Clay County and Northeast Florida highlight our attentive sales team, who listen and never push. They mention that the price they were quoted is what they paid, and that someone remembered their name when they returned. That trend follows over to our service side with advisors who explain why the repair needs to happen before recommending it and service technicians who fix what’s wrong instead of adding unnecessary costs.

Garber Automall is where the Garber family first grew beyond Michigan, and it has anchored our Florida presence since 1986. Eight brands from three manufacturers share one campus on U.S. 17, and every one of them is run on the same belief in doing the right thing — the belief that has led our group to being rated #1 in America for customer service. 75% of Garber leaders are promoted from within, so you can trust the person helping you has been living by our values for years.

We read every review, even the ones we’d rather not get. If something went wrong with a sales or service visit, a manager will reach out directly to make it right — you’re always welcome to call the store first at (904) 236-6884. It’s our commitment to doing the right thing that guarantees you’ll do better at Garber!
