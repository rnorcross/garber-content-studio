About Our Group

A hometown group with a national footprint.

Every Garber dealership, across Michigan, Indiana, Illinois, New York, South Carolina, and Florida, traces its roots back to one Buick showroom in Saginaw, Michigan.

Garber Automotive Group is a third-generation, family-owned company built on more than a century of trust, service, and community commitment. The story began in 1907 when Guy S. Garber began his relationship with Buick as a factory representative, establishing dealerships in Battle Creek and Saginaw, Michigan. In 1912 he purchased the Saginaw dealership and ran Garber Buick until his passing in 1965. His son, Richard Garber Sr., led the business through the early 1970s, and in 1980, his grandson, Richard J. Garber Jr., became president and began expanding the company one dealership at a time. That growth started with Garber Nissan in Saginaw and Garber Ford in Green Cove Springs, Florida, in 1986.

Today, the Garber family of companies includes 25 automotive dealerships across six states, as well as RightWay Auto Sales, Gateway Financial Solutions, and the Saginaw Spirit, an OHL franchise and 2024 Memorial Cup Champions.

While Garber has grown substantially over the years, it has never lost sight of what made the original dealership successful. Every store retains its local identity, its people, and its connection to the communities it serves. Each operates according to the same principles established over a century ago: honest answers, fair pricing, and exceptional service.

That commitment to people extends beyond customers. More than 75% of Garber’s leadership team has been promoted from within, reflecting a culture that invests in its employees and rewards dedication. This approach is why Garber dealerships average a 4.9-star Google rating – the highest of any major dealership group in the United States. It’s clear why generations of customers continue to choose Garber.

Whether you’re purchasing a vehicle, servicing the one you own, or building a career, you’ll do better at Garber.

Our History

A century of automotive pioneering.

In 1907, most of Michigan still thought the horse and buggy were here to stay. Guy S. Garber, a 23-year-old farm-implement salesman, had a different vision – one where the automobile was the future of transportation. His passion caught the eye of Billy Durant, the founder of General Motors, who put him on the road to selling Buicks and opening dealerships across Michigan.

Guy Garber opened a Buick dealership in Saginaw in 1910, bought out the factory’s retail interest in 1912, and by 1913, he held the wholesale territory from Saginaw County all the way north to the Straits of Mackinac. His partnership with GM lasted nearly 60 years, and when he died in 1965, Garber Buick was the last Buick distributor in America.

Where the name first went over a door: R. J. Garber Farm Implements, 414 N. Washington Avenue, Lansing.

Guy Garber at work in the Saginaw office.

Buick leaves the retail business; Guy buys its Saginaw Valley interest. A year later, he is sole owner and one of the Buick Thirteen distributors.

Guy Garber Passes Away at 81

After 58 years working in the auto industry, Guy Garber passed away at 81. Richard Garber Sr., his son, takes over as president and owner of Garber Buick.

1980

Richard J. Garber Jr.

Richard J. Garber Jr., Guy Garber's grandson, becomes president in the company's 73rd year, making him the youngest Buick dealer in the nation.

1986

Garber Automotive Group is born. Garber Nissan in Saginaw, Michigan and Garber Ford in Green Cove Springs, Florida are the first acquisitions.

Community

Giving back has always been at the heart of our business.

Giving back to the communities that support us has been part of the Garber mission since day one. Guy Garber founded the Saginaw Society for Crippled Children, helped organize the Community Chest, which later became the United Way, built the Garber Tennis Courts, and served on the Saginaw County School Board. Three generations on, Richard J. Garber Jr. has led campaigns for the YMCA, the CAN Council, and the Temple Theater, and Garber team members put in thousands of volunteer hours each year.

At every Garber dealership, showing up for the community means sponsoring local organizations, supporting events our teams are passionate about, and giving to causes our customers bring to us. If it matters to the community, it matters to us.
